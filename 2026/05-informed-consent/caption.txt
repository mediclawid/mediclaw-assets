A signed form is not the same as informed consent.

Under Law 17/2023 on Health, every individual health service needs the patient's consent, given after an adequate explanation. For invasive or high-risk procedures, that consent must be in writing and witnessed. Many disputes start not with the treatment itself, but with how consent was obtained.
