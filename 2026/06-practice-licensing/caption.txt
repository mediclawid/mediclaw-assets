Your STR is now valid for life. Your SIP is not.

Under Law 17/2023 on Health and its implementing rules, the Registration Certificate (STR) no longer needs 5-yearly renewal. But the Practice Licence (SIP) still expires after 5 years, covers only one place of practice, and renewal depends on meeting your SKP credits. Many doctors only find out when a renewal stalls or a new clinic shift is already running.
