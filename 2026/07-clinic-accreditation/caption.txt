Accredited once is not accredited forever.

Under MoH Regulation 34/2022, every clinic, health laboratory, Puskesmas and independent doctor's or dentist's practice must be accredited, whether or not it works with BPJS. The first accreditation is due within 2 years of operating, the certificate lasts 5 years, and re-accreditation must be applied for at least 3 months before it expires. Many facilities only notice when the certificate has already lapsed.
