Clinic type

Decide: primary or main clinic?

A Klinik Pratama provides basic medical services. A Klinik Utama provides specialist services and needs specialist doctors on staff.

Your service plan drives everything else: staffing, equipment, licensing scope and even who may invest.

GR 28/2024 · MoH Reg. 11/2025

02
Entity & KBLI

Set up the entity with the right KBLI

Private clinics fall under KBLI 86105. Licensing runs through OSS-RBA under GR 28/2025, which replaced GR 5/2021.

Make sure the KBLI in your deed of establishment matches the one in OSS. A mismatch can stall the whole application.

GR 28/2025 on Risk-Based Business Licensing

Foreign investment

Foreign investor? Check the scope first

Foreign ownership is treated differently for primary and main clinics, and a PT PMA must meet minimum investment rules per KBLI per location.

Confirm the exact OSS business scope open to foreign capital before you sign a shareholders' or JV agreement.

GR 28/2025 · Investment Positive List · MoH Reg. 11/2025

04
Licensing

Your NIB is not your clinic licence

The NIB only identifies your business. You still need the clinic business licence, issued once you meet the standards, plus:

  • Spatial suitability (KKPR)
  • Environmental approval
  • Building approval (PBG) & SLF
GR 28/2025 · MoH Reg. 11/2025

05
People

No SIP, no practice. No exceptions.

The clinic head must be a doctor or dentist, and every medical and health worker needs a valid STR and SIP before treating patients.

Employing staff without a SIP is a criminal offence: up to 5 years in prison or a fine of up to IDR 500 million.

Law 17/2023, Art. 312(c) & 442 · MoH Reg. 11/2025

After opening

Opening day is not the finish line

  • Register the facility in the MoH facility registry
  • Run Electronic Medical Records from day one
  • Get accredited within 2 years of your first licence
