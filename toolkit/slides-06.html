Registration

Your STR is now valid for life

The Registration Certificate (STR) no longer needs renewal every 5 years. For Indonesian practitioners, it is issued once and remains valid for life.

Lifetime registration is not a lifetime licence to practise. That is what the SIP is for.

Law 17/2023 on Health; MoH Reg. 13/2025

Practice licence

But your SIP lasts 5

The SIP still lasts 5 years

nbsp;years

To practise, you need a Practice Licence (SIP). It requires a valid STR and a place of practice, and is valid for 5 years, renewable.

SIPs issued before the new rules stay valid until they expire. Diary your expiry date now.

Law 17/2023 on Health; MoH Reg. 13/2025

03
Practice places

One SIP, one place. Three places max.

Each SIP is valid for one place of practice only. Doctors and dentists may practise in up to three places, so three places means three SIPs.

Adding a clinic shift? Get the SIP for that location before your first patient.

GR 28/2024, Art. 682(2); MoH Reg. 13/2025

04
Renewal

Renewal runs on SKP credits

To renew a SIP, you must show you have met the required professional credit units (SKP) for continuing competence.

Check your SKP balance in the MoH system well before renewal. Shortfalls are hard to fix in a month.

MoH Reg. 13/2025 (implementing GR 28/2024)

05
Who issues it

Your SIP comes from the regency or city

The SIP is issued by the regency/city government where you practise, through its health office or one-stop service office.

Local governments may not add requirements beyond the national rules. Ask for the legal basis of any extra demand.

Law 17/2023 on Health; MoH Reg. 13/2025

06
For hospitals & clinics

No valid SIP, no practice

Practising without a SIP is a violation, and giving the impression of holding an STR/SIP you do not have can mean up to 5 years in prison or a Rp500 million fine.

Facilities: track every practitioner's SIP expiry and location in your credentialing file.

Law 17/2023 on Health, Art. 441 jo. Art. 312
